# Travel and Expense Policy

Applies to all Quokka Robotics employees. Effective 1 January 2026.

## Approval thresholds

- Up to EUR 250 per expense: no pre-approval needed, submit receipt within 30 days.
- EUR 250 to EUR 2,000: approval by your direct manager before the spend.
- Above EUR 2,000: approval by your department head and Finance before the spend.
- Any expense submitted later than 60 days after it was incurred is not reimbursed.

## Travel

- Trains are preferred over flights for trips under 5 hours door to door.
- Economy class for flights under 6 hours; premium economy allowed for longer flights.
- Hotels: up to EUR 180 per night in standard cities, EUR 260 per night in Tier 1 cities (London, Paris, Zurich, New York, San Francisco, Tokyo).
- Mileage for private cars: EUR 0.43 per km.

## Meals and per diem

Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to the daily per diem for the city:

| City category | Daily meal limit | Single meal limit |
|---------------|------------------|-------------------|
| Standard      | EUR 60           | EUR 35            |
| Tier 1        | EUR 95           | EUR 55            |

Customer entertainment (a meal with a customer present) follows different rules: up to EUR 90 per person including the customer, maximum four Quokka employees per customer meal, and the customer's name and company must be on the claim. Alcohol is reimbursed only during customer entertainment.

## Not reimbursed

Traffic fines, minibar, in-flight Wi-Fi on flights under 3 hours, personal entertainment, and lost-item replacements.

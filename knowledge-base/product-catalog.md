# Quokka Robotics Product Catalog (2026)

Quokka Robotics is a fictional company that builds autonomous robots for warehouses and industrial inspection. All products are managed through the Quokka Fleet Controller (QFC), a cloud service.

## QR-1 "Wombat" Warehouse Picker

The Wombat is a wheeled picking robot for shelving up to 2.4 m high.

- Payload: 25 kg per pick, 120 kg tote capacity
- Speed: 1.8 m/s loaded, 2.5 m/s empty
- Battery: 6.5 hours of continuous operation, 70 minute fast charge to 80%
- Navigation: lidar + ceiling fiducials, no floor markings required
- List price: EUR 38,500 per unit, or EUR 1,150 per unit per month on Robot-as-a-Service (RaaS)
- Minimum fleet size for RaaS: 5 units

## QR-3 "Numbat" Pallet Mover

The Numbat moves full pallets between docks and racking.

- Payload: 1,200 kg
- Speed: 1.2 m/s loaded
- Battery: 9 hours, swappable battery packs (swap takes 3 minutes)
- Safety: certified to ISO 3691-4
- List price: EUR 64,000 per unit, or EUR 1,900 per unit per month on RaaS

## QR-5 "Platypus" Inspection Drone

The Platypus is an indoor drone for inventory counting and infrastructure inspection.

- Flight time: 28 minutes, automatic return-to-dock at 20% battery
- Sensors: 48 MP camera, thermal camera, RFID reader (range 6 m)
- Inventory scan rate: up to 9,000 pallet locations per hour
- List price: EUR 22,000 per unit, or EUR 690 per unit per month on RaaS
- Not certified for outdoor use; maximum wind tolerance is irrelevant indoors but the drone refuses to launch above 35 degrees Celsius ambient temperature.
